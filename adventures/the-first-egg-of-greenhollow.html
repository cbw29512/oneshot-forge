The First Egg of Greenhollow

Greenhollow's spring hunt begins with ninety-nine painted eggs and one First Egg meant to return shared seed to the earth. This year the real egg was replaced with a jeweled replica—and sunrise stopped.

  • Holiday egg hunt with an actual agricultural purpose.
  • Shared-seed mystery with no single point of failure.
  • Old orchard, conservatory, and Dawn Field maps.
  • Three endings: restore the rite, build a seed bank, or end the enchantment.
