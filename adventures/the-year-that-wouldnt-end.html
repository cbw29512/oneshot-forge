The Year That Wouldn't End

Yearturn burned its old ledger at midnight so everyone could begin again. This year the unresolved promises were burned too—and the old year refuses to leave.

  • Visible twelfth-bell countdown.
  • Short repeating-time echoes that pressure scenes without unrestricted time travel.
  • Public and private obligations reconstructed through redundant evidence.
  • Three endings: carry obligations forward, forgive with acknowledgment, or end supernatural enforcement.
