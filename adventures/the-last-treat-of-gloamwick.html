The Last Treat of Gloamwick

During Gloamwick's masked lantern parade, three children vanish and every doorstep's final sweet disappears before midnight. The pumpkin-headed figure seen leading the children away may be the only thing keeping them alive.

  • A visible four-step midnight bell clock.
  • A missing-child mystery with no single point of failure.
  • A physical Halloween custom puzzle with fail-forward clues.
  • A false-monster reversal the players can prove through people and physical evidence.
  • Three complete endings: restore the offering, remember the forgotten dead, or destroy the threat by force.

Why this is a Halloween adventure

The holiday tradition is the mechanism.

Last treat

Every home leaves one final sweet beneath its lantern until midnight. Removing them early breaks the town's old protection.

Masks

The true threat forms from forgotten mourning masks, while the frightening pumpkin-headed figure is protecting the missing children.

Midnight

The final bell is a visible table clock that pushes the investigation into a timed town-square finale.

Your ending

Players decide whether next year's Night of Masks is about offerings, remembrance, or starting over without supernatural protection.
