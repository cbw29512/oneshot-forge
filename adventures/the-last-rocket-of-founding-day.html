The Last Rocket of Founding Day

Seven signal rockets are meant to close Brightwater's civic celebration. Someone bent the last launch rack, old equipment was reused, and the final rocket now points at a packed harbor.

  • Visible seven-signal countdown.
  • Multiple causes: sabotage, unsafe equipment, and suppressed warnings.
  • Harbor evacuation work changes final consequences.
  • Three endings: fire safely, rewrite the ritual, or retire the rockets.
