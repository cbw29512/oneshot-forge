The Bell Beneath the Mere

A drowned bell has started ringing beneath perfectly still water. Tamsin Reed vanished while investigating it. The dead bellkeeper is not calling victims down—she is still sounding a warning nobody answered.

  • Cross the Silent Reeds by fighting or distracting vibration-hunting skimmers.
  • Read a LOW / HIGH / LOW warning sequence from physical water-line clues.
  • Rescue Tamsin through the flooded stair or emergency air shaft as the Mere rises.
  • Recover the founder archive, then release Ysra, silence the bell, or preserve its warning.
