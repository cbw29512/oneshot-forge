The Wedding That Never Ends

Lady Seraphine Vale is marched toward a vow she did not choose. The privateer she loved wears a stolen shape. The day on Veyra resets because a lost octopus god, Serevath, still has one law left: a broken tide returns to the morning it left. The draft was written for 4–6. This table is tuned for 4, with a smaller wave at 3 and extra hands at 5 and 6.

  • Land, hear the warning, and follow the ribbon lane to a wedding that has already happened.
  • Sort the chapel verse, gather first-keel wood, and breathe the wreck by more than one route.
  • Break the captain's coat. Leave the Bell Avatar alone. A second touch, or the bride's refusal, ends the theft.
  • If the table strikes the god anyway, the party dies and wakes on the ferry. That is the lesson, not the ending.

What Veyra remembers: whether the oath broke by two strikes or by Seraphine's refusal, whether the king said the theft out loud, and that Serevath was not killed. How the night runs

The god explains the wipe. The wood ends the wedding.

Serevath

An original lost god. The Gargantuan octopus is that god's last body, homebrew, CR 20, with a 2014 block and a 2024 block. It is not a fight to win.

The return

A death dealt by the avatar rewinds the island to the ferry at three in the afternoon. Memories stay. The morning body comes back. The same lie will not work twice.

Two ways through

First-keel wood, twice, or Seraphine breaking the oath ring and refusing the marriage. New timber does nothing.

Both editions

Six level-5 pregens in the 2014 SRD and six rebuilt for the 2024 SRD. Underwater, smites, and the return are labeled by edition.
