The Ashen Bridge

The Night Spring resurfaces under Oakhaven's east road just as a merchant caravan is stranded on a failing bridge. Six civilians. Two wagons. Four integrity. The rescue clock starts when you step onto the span.

  • Mix an ash-lime seal from a player-facing maintenance clue.
  • Set a guide line, dump dangerous cargo, and decide whether the medicine wagon is worth the actions.
  • Move civilians in pairs while the bridge loses integrity every round.
  • Saving every person counts as success even if the bridge itself is lost.

What Oakhaven remembers: who the party saved when lives, cargo, medicine, and infrastructure could not all be treated as equal priorities. Free to play while we finish testing. It will cost $0.99 when it launches.

Zero-prep rescue board

Track five things. Run the whole finale.

Integrity 4

Lose one each round until the eastern pier is sealed.

Civilians 6

Successful guide actions move two; failure still moves one.

Heavy cargo

Dump it before the first Pressure Break or pay an extra integrity.

Medicine wagon

Optional. Saving it earns a bonus reward but never outranks lives.
