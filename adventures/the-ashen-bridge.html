The Ashen Bridge

The Night Spring resurfaces under Oakhaven's east road just as a merchant caravan is stranded on a failing bridge. Six civilians. Two wagons. Four integrity. The rescue clock starts when you step onto the span.

  • Mix an ash-lime seal from a player-facing maintenance clue.
  • Set a guide line, dump dangerous cargo, and decide whether the medicine wagon is worth the actions.
  • Move civilians in pairs while the bridge loses integrity every round.
  • Saving every person counts as success even if the bridge itself is lost.

What Oakhaven remembers: who the party saved when lives, cargo, medicine, and infrastructure could not all be treated as equal priorities. $0.99 target price. Checkout remains closed until release approval and verified entitlement are configured.
