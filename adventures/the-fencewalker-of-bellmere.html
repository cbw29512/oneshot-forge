The Fencewalker of Bellmere

Something tall has been walking Bellmere's fence rails and stealing iron nails before a hard storm. The villagers think they have a monster problem. Following the stolen iron reveals something stranger: the creature may be the only one who knows the road is about to collapse.

  • Track impossible prints that appear on fence rails but not in the mud below.
  • Decide whether to hunt, signal, follow, or help a cryptid that cannot explain itself in words.
  • Use the stolen iron, timber, stone, and local tools in a three-job fissure repair.
  • Choose whether Bellmere saves its road, recovers its property, protects the creature, or accepts a controlled collapse.

What Bellmere remembers: whether the heroes killed the “monster,” listened to its warning, and what they chose to save when the storm arrived. $0.99 target price. Checkout remains closed until release approval and verified entitlement are configured.

Cryptid mystery structure

The sightings are real. The assumption is wrong.

Storm 0–3

Failed risky shortcuts and destroyed braces bring the storm closer without locking progress.

Three knocks

The Fencewalker's strange behavior becomes a readable warning pattern instead of random monster flavor.

Three repair jobs

Divert runoff, brace the roof, and pin the main seam using literal materials visible at the table.

No required kill

The finale is about the road, the villagers, and the stolen iron. Killing the cryptid never solves the real problem.
