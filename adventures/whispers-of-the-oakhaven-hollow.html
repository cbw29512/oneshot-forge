Whispers of the Oakhaven Hollow

Old Man Kaelen needs four vials of Gloom-Lily Nectar. The Whispering Hollow is choking around Malakor, the druid who tried to take the Heart Tree’s corruption into himself. He is dangerous now—but lucid pieces of the man Kaelen knew are still fighting underneath.

  • Fight through or bypass the Bramble Guard, including a bear that can be freed instead of killed.
  • Solve Decay → Balance → Growth, or use Arcana/Religion to force the Sunken Shrine open with a real fail-forward cost.
  • Break the Heart Tree Core with fire or radiant power, then defeat Malakor or cleanse him alive.
  • A visible three-step Shroud clock changes the harvest and the fate of the grove.

Level 2 party tie-ins

Four roles fit the Hollow.

Fighter

Hold the thorn lane, protect cleansing attempts, and break the bear's living collar.

Rogue

Cross hazardous roots, read shrine mechanisms, and recover hidden offerings.

Cleric

Radiant damage can expose the Core; Religion can drive the cleansing ritual.

Wizard / Sorcerer

Area control punishes clustered thorn-shrubs; Arcana can operate the Moonstone Pillars.
