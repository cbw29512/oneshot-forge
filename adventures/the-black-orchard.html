The Black Orchard

A missing surveyor entered the orchard looking for wardstone. The trees are black, the apples are darker, and the keeper insists the fruit is not a curse—it is containment.

  • Disable or bypass rag-stitched orchard wardens instead of defaulting to destruction.
  • Solve a physical ash/rain/black-fruit offering puzzle with immediate fail-forward clues.
  • Rescue the surveyor who accidentally destabilized the living wardstone seam.
  • Choose who gets one living shard: the orchard, Oakhaven, or both through a risky living graft.
