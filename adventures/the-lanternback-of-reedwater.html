The Lanternback of Reedwater

Three skiffs have overturned. Two goats are missing. A broad creature with six blue-green lights along its back appears wherever Reedwater turns black. Everyone calls it the cause. The evidence says otherwise.

  • Interview witnesses whose stories disagree in useful ways.
  • Compare clean and contaminated water and test what the creature actually does.
  • Find a missing fisher alive among animals the “monster” supposedly took.
  • Trace the real danger to a leaking dye cistern beneath the abandoned Sableworks.

What Reedwater remembers: whether the heroes proved the cryptid was a rescuer, made the river safe, and changed a local monster story into a warning people understand. Free to play while we finish testing. It will cost $0.99 when it launches.

Cryptid Case structure

The monster story falls apart one piece of evidence at a time.

Witness contradiction

Bram insists the creature turned his skiff toward shore rather than attacking it.

Test the theory

Players can compare water samples and deliberately observe how the Lanternback reacts to danger.

Missing person reveal

Corin is alive on a dry reed island with other creatures the Lanternback pulled from contaminated water.

No boss fight

The finale is containment, rescue, and waterway closure. Fighting the cryptid never fixes Reedwater.
