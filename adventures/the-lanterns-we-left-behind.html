The Lanterns We Left Behind

Bellmere's Lanterns of Return once left blank lanterns for the unnamed, missing, and unidentified dead. Those lanterns were removed—and now pale Road-Bearers are walking the abandoned north road with spirits who no longer have a place in the ceremony.

  • Visible River of Light countdown and a ghost-road investigation.
  • Remembrance mystery with multiple independent clue routes.
  • Escort, frightened, psychic-pressure, and spirit encounters that serve the story.
  • Three endings: restore blank lanterns, create a permanent memorial registry, or close the ghost road.

Free public playtest. Use the browser runner or printable materials; no checkout is required.
