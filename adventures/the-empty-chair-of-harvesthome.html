The Empty Chair of Harvesthome

Harvesthome begins its feast with one chair missing, one wagon of grain unaccounted for, and a cold wind that steals food from every table where nobody kept a place for a stranger.

  • Visible Final Toast clock.
  • A real material shortage plus a supernatural consequence.
  • A falsely accused outer-farm family with redundant evidence proving innocence.
  • A mill hoard that forces an actual distribution decision.
  • Three complete endings: keep the chair, replace it with a public sharing compact, or end the old pact entirely.

Free public playtest. No checkout is required.
