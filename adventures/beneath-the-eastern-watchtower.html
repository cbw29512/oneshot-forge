Beneath the Eastern Watchtower

The map from the Hollow points under Oakhaven's eastern watchtower. Below the stone is a forgotten civic ward, a flooded sally tunnel, a bellkeeper's pressure puzzle, and a machine venting black splinters into the town's roots.

  • Fight or bypass Stone-Sump Crawlers in a collapsing flooded undercroft.
  • Balance a real 5 / 3 / 1 pressure puzzle with visible fail-forward consequences.
  • Repair three systems under live pressure instead of defaulting to a boss fight.
  • Destroying the core is legal—but the party knows it will cost Oakhaven its eastern watchtower.

What Oakhaven remembers: whether the party preserved the old eastern ward or chose to save the town by sacrificing its watchtower. $0.99 target price. Checkout remains closed until release approval and verified entitlement are configured.

What makes this one different

The finale is a failing machine, not a bag of hit points.

Three repair jobs

Salt Gate, Bell Ground, and Root Vent can be handled in parallel by different characters.

Pressure clock

Every delay can cost the watchtower without blocking the adventure.

Destructive shortcut

Smashing the core works immediately, but permanently removes Oakhaven's old ward.

Series hook

The sealed ledger points north to the Black Orchard for Module 04.
