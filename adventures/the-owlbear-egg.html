The Owlbear Egg

Briar Hollow is starving. A hunter has brought back a living owlbear egg as food, and the village must decide what desperation permits. Then its gaunt father crashes into town, mad with hunger.

  • Feed the father your one-day rations and he eats, calms, and wanders off. Fight him and his sick meat is useless.
  • If he dies, a mother owlbear roughly twice his bulk screams from the ridge.
  • Cook the egg, let it hatch, return it to the wild, or take responsibility for another solution. No moral answer is scored as correct.
