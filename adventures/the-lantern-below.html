The Lantern Below

A village lantern that never needed oil has gone dark. Recover the stolen Emberglass before moonrise — then decide whether the thief was trying to save the village from what the lantern imprisoned.

  • Meaningful routes change the danger, clues, resources, and finale position, then reconverge on the same crisis.
  • Zero prep: open the runner and follow the ordered stage cards from hook to resolution.
  • Required revelations have backup delivery, so one failed check or skipped route cannot break the adventure.
  • Every active stage keeps read-aloud, GM notes, NPC cues, DCs, encounters, clues, rewards, tactical callouts, pacing, and next choices together.
