The Watch That Did Not Come Back

Captain Brune Holt sent six watchmen up the ridge on the dawn round. Three nights later their lanterns are still walking the skyline — and not one man has come home. You have until dawn, when the lights turn around.

  • Five of the six are already dead and marching a round that will not end. One is still alive — find out which lantern still burns warm before the round turns.
  • The dead watch are still soldiers. Carry the captain's seal and the countersign and you can order them home without a fight.
  • Bring them home, cut the warden's tally, or take their post — every ending has a cost, and none is scored as the right one.
