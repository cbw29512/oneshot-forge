The Gift That Never Arrived

Wintermere opened its presents before the traditional First Gift reached an isolated household. Now opened gifts are freezing shut again and the village hearths are losing heat.

  • Visible last-bell clock.
  • A real winter delivery and rescue, not symbolic gift wrapping.
  • A snow-road obstacle with multiple safe approaches.
  • A guardian that looks threatening but is enforcing the promise the town asked it to keep.
  • Three endings: finish the old custom, replace it with a winter-aid system, or break the pact entirely.
