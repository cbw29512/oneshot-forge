The Graduation That Opened the Sky

Ninefold Academy's graduation is supposed to close the planar observatory for another year. One student capstone was erased from the ceremony—and the ninth beacon opens the sky instead.

  • Level-9-friendly design that respects flight, teleportation, dispelling, and 5th-level utility magic.
  • Three independent copies of the missing grounding work.
  • Student rescue jobs that high-level spells can solve legitimately.
  • Three endings: restore the capstone, reform observatory governance, or disconnect graduation from the planar system.
