How a session goes

  1. Open the pack. Browser or downloaded file.
  2. Hand out pregens only if someone needs a character. Everyone else uses their own.
  3. Read the boxed text. Checks and stat blocks sit under it. Do not hunt a PDF.
  4. End on the choice the table made. The pack has the endings written.
