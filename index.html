SPECIAL PLAYTEST BOXED SET · LEVEL 0 · 7 SESSIONS · 4–6 PLAYERS

From Zero to Hero: play through character creation.

This is the longer-form exception to the one-shot catalog. Start at age 12 with six 9s and no class, then grow up in Hearthcross across seven sessions of chores, dances, competitions, apprenticeship, travel, a storm, and graduation. Tod, Merlon, Halley, Mira, Fen, and Lark already have families, fears, ambitions, and relationships before the first roll; every important win and loss then becomes part of the scrapbook that builds the Level-1 character.

Complete playtest kit · Not for sale · Hearthcross lies about a day’s ride from Hearthford, with the old road continuing east toward Oakhaven. Level-0 homebrew ends at graduation, then the character hands off to the chosen 2014 or 2024 rules edition.
