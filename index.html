Before game night

Can I run it from the website or print it?

Both. Start adventure opens the GM runner. Each adventure page also links to its printable module and print center; use your browser’s Save as PDF option to keep a copy.

Which version of 5e does it support?

Choose 2014 SRD or 2024 SRD from the rules toggle, then print character sheets for that edition. Use one edition at your table.

What is free right now?

The three catalog adventures are free previews, including their available print resources and pregens. Paid releases have a planned price of $0.99 and open after approval and checkout setup.
