DM Quick Sheet — Final Clue Flow (v1.2)

Right to OwlBear Arms v1.2 · DM only · Chronology and procedures: DM Book Part 1, §1 and §11

  • Mayor Tessa: armory empty, raiders coming by sundown, south gate vulnerable. Points to the armory, Halven, Edra, and village defenses.
  • Armory: picked lock, owlbear tracks, intellect residue, possible Ilyra Voss clue, and a second watch-boot trail heading south. Points to the owlbear, the mantle's origin, and the traitor.
  • Halven: looks guilty, admits he slept, and — if treated with mercy — reveals Edra leaving the village at odd hours toward the south road. Points to Edra.
  • Nim: saw the intelligent owlbear in its mantle, smoke, and scouts. Points to Old Cobb and the forest path.
  • Old Cobb: gives the survivor map. Went into the woods with Tomas Vell two days ago and came back alone. If pressed: blade wounds, coat cut open — not claws. Points to Tomas's death site, Edra's motive, and the lair approach.
  • Tomas's death site (near the lair edge): humanoid/raider involvement, not owlbear mauling. Points away from Grubbleclaw and toward Edra's raider contact.
  • Skitters: knows dusk timing, rough numbers, and the egg objective if protected; has seen the iron-haired woman leave patrol times on the south road. Points to the egg raid, the Ash Shaman, and Edra.
  • Edra: a genuinely useful watch captain who sold the raiders patrol routes three months ago and has been hiding it ever since — including feeding her brother Tomas false leads. She does not know he is dead. Her reveal can happen before the battle or during the gate breach.
  • Grubbleclaw: confirms the south-gate intel came from inside, explains the Ilyra/mantle origin, and treats the weapons as a legal property dispute — not proof he killed anyone. Points to alliance or conflict.
  • Finale: the raid hits the village while an egg team moves on the nest in Wave 3. Run it with the prep clock, Defense Points, Routes A–E, and the waves in Part 1, §2–6.
Do not reveal early: eggs, nest, Edra as traitor, Halven as red herring, Tomas's death, or the raiders' egg objective. Let play earn those reveals (Part 1, §11).
