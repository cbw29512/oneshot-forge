RIGHT TO OWLBEAR ARMS - COMPLETE TABLE-READY BUNDLE

A one-shot for 5 level-6 players. Estimated runtime: 5-7 hours.

START HERE
1. Read 01_Adventure/Right_To_Bear_Arms_Adventure.pdf.
2. Print one hero per player from 02_Pregenerated_Heroes.
3. Keep the DM maps in 03_Maps hidden; Old Cobb's survivor map is player-safe.
4. Print 04_Handouts and reveal the Accord/Writ only if play earns them.
5. NPC cards in 05_NPC_Cards are designed for duplex printing (flip on short edge).
6. 06_DM_Aids contains the Grubbleclaw visual and final clue-flow sheet.

CONTENTS
- Complete 29-page v1.1 adventure / DM packet
- New DM Fast Start + expanded DM Cheat Sheet inside the adventure
- 5 ready-to-play level-6 pregenerated heroes
- Old Cobb survivor map (player-safe)
- Brindlebarrow village defense map (DM)
- Owlbear lair full-layout map (DM)
- Brindlebarrow Nest Accord + Writ of Passage player handouts
- Finished duplex NPC card set
- Magistrate Grubbleclaw DM visual
- Final clue-flow DM sheet
- Editable DOCX source for the adventure

PRINTING
- PDFs: print at 100% / Actual Size unless your printer requires Fit for margin safety.
- NPC cards: duplex, flip on short edge.
- Keep DM-only maps/cards out of player view.
