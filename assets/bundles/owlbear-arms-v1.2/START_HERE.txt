RIGHT TO OWLBEAR ARMS - COMPLETE TABLE-READY BUNDLE - VERSION 1.2

A one-shot for 3-6 level-6 players (5 heroes provided). Estimated runtime: 5-7 hours.
Rules: 2014 rules, SRD 5.1.

START HERE
1. Read 01_Adventure/Right_To_Bear_Arms_Adventure_v1.2.pdf.
   Part 1 (Rules of Play) holds the locked chronology, the four-hour prep clock,
   Defense Points, Routes A-E, wave rosters with XP math, 3-6 player scaling,
   every enemy block, and Grubbleclaw's combat block.
   Part 2 (Scenes, NPCs & Dialogue) holds every scene, NPC, and ending.
   If anything in Part 2 seems to differ from Part 1, Part 1 wins.
2. Print one hero per player from 02_Pregenerated_Heroes (SRD 5.1 only).
3. Keep the DM maps in 03_Maps hidden; Old Cobb's survivor map is player-safe.
4. Print 04_Handouts and reveal the Accord/Writ only if play earns them.
5. NPC cards in 05_NPC_Cards are designed for duplex printing (flip on short edge).
6. 06_DM_Aids contains the Grubbleclaw visual and the v1.2 clue-flow sheet.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.2
- One locked chronology: Tomas survived the logging-camp raid and died in the
  woods two days ago at raider hands; Edra's motive is concealing her collaboration.
- One four-hour preparation clock; Defense Points 4 (minimum 0, maximum 6).
- Complete final-defense procedure (Routes A-E), wave timing, and travel.
- Reproducible SRD-sourced enemy rosters and XP math; scaling for 3-6 players.
- Grubbleclaw v1.2 combat block; custom items clearly labelled CUSTOM.
- Five SRD 5.1-only heroes with complete sheets and spell references.
- NPC cards, clue-flow sheet, and handouts synchronized with the new chronology.

PRINTING
- PDFs: print at 100% / Actual Size unless your printer requires Fit for margin safety.
- NPC cards: duplex, flip on short edge.
- Keep DM-only maps/cards out of player view.
