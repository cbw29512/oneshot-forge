# What actually makes a high-quality one-shot

Not more adventures. One night a stranger can run without rewriting it.

## The bar
1. **One image.** Pitch in a sentence. The lantern. The well. If you need a paragraph, it is not ready.
2. **A physical clock.** Moonrise, the well going black — something the table can see, not “when you feel like it.”
3. **A situation, not a plot.** Factions, a failing blessing, a choice. The players decide the ending. At least two legal finales.
4. **Three ways to every required fact.** One of them is a thing in the world (notes, tracks, a wall). Killing the quest giver cannot stop the night.
5. **Numbers on the same card as the boxed text.** AC, HP, DC, bypass. If the DM has to open another tab, we failed.
6. **Combat is a job.** Hold the ritual, smash the glass, bargain. A bag of hit points is homework.
7. **Prepared pays, rush costs, nobody is stuck.** Extra gold and a weaker finale if they did the work. Still finish if they didn’t.
8. **Pregens that are real sheets.** Every feature, every spell effect, every skill. Hand it over and roll. Not a class name and an AC.
9. **Maps for every fight.** Grid and distances. A player handout of the town with no secrets on it.
10. **Both legal editions.** 2014 SRD and 2024 SRD 5.2.1, same night, one toggle. Do not mix features across editions.
11. **Specifics.** Named people, distances, what the room smells like. Cut generic “guards.”
12. **Cut 20%.** Then stop. In the curated lane, one strong authored episode beats rushed filler. In the Forge Farm lane, generation volume is allowed, but only candidates that clear the constitution's release gates may ship.

## This product
See `docs/ONESHOT_RECIPE.md` for the session shape (five rooms, trap, puzzle, talk-out, SRD XP budget). That file is mandatory for every authored night.

## Forge Farm note
For generated adventures, `docs/FORGE_CONSTITUTION.md` is authoritative. This quality bar remains mandatory, but it must not be used to collapse the farm back into a hand-authored-only product.

## Locked production standards

- Pregens must satisfy `docs/PREGEN_PRINT_STANDARD.md`.
- Read-aloud, NPC/monster cards, location descriptions, and scene transitions must satisfy `docs/PRESENTATION_STANDARD.md`.
- Public catalog, landing-page, GM-runner, module, print, readability, resume, and deployment behavior must satisfy `docs/PRODUCTION_GAMEPLAY_STANDARD.md`.
- These are release gates, not optional style guidance.
