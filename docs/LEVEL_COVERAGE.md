# One-Shot Level Coverage Ladder

New authored one-shots should deliberately cover every character level from 1 through 20.

## Rule

- Start at Level 1 and fill upward one level at a time.
- Do not assign a second new adventure to a level until Levels 1–20 each have at least one release-quality entry.
- Existing legacy/review adventures keep their authored levels; this ladder governs new work from this point forward.
- Every ladder adventure remains standalone. A group may also play the ladder as a loose level-by-level progression.
- The adventure must be designed for its actual level. Do not merely increase hit points.
- Re-audit encounter pressure, save DCs, damage, mobility, spell access, class resources, treasure, pregens, and finale mechanics at each level.
- 2014 and 2024 pregens remain separate and must be legal for the selected edition.
