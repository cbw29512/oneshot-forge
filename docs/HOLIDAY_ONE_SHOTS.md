# Holiday One-Shots

Holiday adventures are a dedicated 99¢ One-Shots product line. They use the same release standards as every other adventure; seasonal theme never excuses weaker mechanics or a thinner story.

## Product promise

A holiday one-shot should give a group a complete themed game night that feels specific to the holiday without requiring the DM to rewrite encounters, invent connective tissue, or own an unrelated book.

Each release still needs:
- a one-sentence pitch and visible clock;
- a complete DM runner;
- 2014 and 2024 rules support kept separate;
- real pregens for the selected edition;
- maps for every fight;
- printable NPC/monster cards and player handouts where useful;
- a quick-run/cheat sheet;
- at least two legal endings;
- fail-forward checks;
- three independent sources for every required revelation;
- at least one physical/environmental backup for every required revelation;
- required revelations surfaced in at least two distinct scenes;
- no required information that disappears with one NPC;
- a noncombat or de-escalation route when the fiction reasonably permits one.

## Level ladder

Holiday releases follow the catalog-wide 1→20 progression in `docs/LEVEL_COVERAGE.md`. The next new holiday or non-holiday one-shot takes the next unused level in that ledger.

## Holiday identity

The holiday must matter to the adventure's situation, not just its decoration.

A release should have at least three of:
1. a holiday-specific social ritual, tradition, or community expectation;
2. a holiday-specific clock or deadline;
3. a holiday-specific object, location, food, gift, celebration, or public event that affects play;
4. a moral choice tied to the meaning of the holiday;
5. an encounter or puzzle whose logic grows naturally from the holiday;
6. an ending that changes the celebration itself.

Do not make “normal dungeon, but pumpkins/snow/hearts” products.

## Planned lane

Priority order is seasonal rather than permanent. Release only after the normal quality gates pass.

| Season | Working lane | Useful tones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Halloween | first priority | spooky fun, folk horror, haunted celebration, trick-or-treat mystery |
| Thanksgiving / harvest | next | gratitude, hunger, harvest bargain, family/community tension |
| Winter / Christmas | next | gifts, lost celebration, winter rescue, generosity, folklore |
| New Year | later | countdown, unfinished promises, old-year/new-year choice |
| Valentine | later | bonds, mistaken devotion, cursed promises, friendship/romance-safe |
| Spring / Easter-style | later | renewal, eggs, spring festival, missing dawn ritual |
| Independence / summer festival | later | fireworks, town celebration, sabotage, rescue |
| Birthday | existing special occasion line | celebration, friendship, wish, personalized hero |

Holiday products do not need to belong to Quiet Seals. They may be fully standalone as long as the runner, rules edition, pregens, and downloadable support remain complete.

## First build target

The first new holiday adventure is Halloween and occupies Level 1 in the 1–20 ladder. The next new one-shot should target Level 2.

Design target:
- 3–4 hours;
- 4 characters by default, scalable to 3–6;
- clear Halloween premise within the first ten minutes;
- spooky but playable for a broad table rather than gore-dependent;
- one memorable physical clock;
- at least one social problem, one interactive obstacle, and one finale with multiple legal resolutions;
- no single point of failure;
- player choices determine what the town is celebrating by the ending.

The concept is not locked in this document. Story bones should be chosen only after comparing several strong premises against the normal release bar.
