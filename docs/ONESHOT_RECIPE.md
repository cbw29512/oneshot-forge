# One-shot recipe (every Quiet Seals night)

Do not invent a new shape. This is the tried-and-true path. A night that skips a beat is homework.

## The path (five rooms, four hours)

Johnn Four’s five-room dungeon, used as a session shape — not as a railroad.

| Beat | Job | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Entrance / guardian | Something in the way. Fight, sneak, or talk. | 25–40 min |
| 2. Puzzle or roleplay | A lock, a person, a choice. Numbers on the card. | 25–40 min |
| 3. Trick / setback | The trap. Failure still moves the night (fail-forward). | 15–25 min |
| 4. Climax | A job, not a bag of HP. Talk-out is legal unless the thing cannot hear. | 40–50 min |
| 5. Reward / revelation | Treasure **and** a fact that follows them back to Hearthford. | 15–20 min |

Hook in the first ten minutes. Clock on the table. Two or more legal endings. Three sources for every required fact. Maps for every fight.

## Must be on the page

- **One trap** with trigger, save/DC, damage or condition, and fail-forward.
- **One puzzle** with the solution printed for the GM, a skill bypass, and fail-forward.
- **One talk-out.** At least one encounter lists a DC and what the NPC/creature wants. No mandatory BBEG. If they talk, the night still ends.
- **Combat budget** from SRD 5.2.1 (CC-BY), not copied DMG 2014 charts. Four 4th-level characters:

| Difficulty | XP budget |
|---|---|
| Low | 1,000 (250 × 4) |
| Moderate | 1,500 (375 × 4) |
| High | 2,000 (500 × 4) |

Spend monster XP from SRD stat blocks. Original creatures: pick a CR, use that CR’s XP from the SRD table (CR 1 = 200, CR 2 = 450, CR 3 = 700, CR 4 = 1,100). Neutralizing without a kill still awards the XP.

If the table owns a DMG, they may also roll its random dungeon/encounter charts. We do not reprint those charts.

## Flexible, not sloppy

- Bypass is written. Culvert, disguise, bribe, ritual.
- Killing the giver does not stop the night.
- Horror nights still have a talk-out (bargain, exorcism, walking away with a cost).
- Classic-fantasy nights still have a trap and a puzzle.

## Genre mix (public-domain DNA, not WotC plots)

We steal structure from folktale and myth, never trade dress.

| Night | Genre | Tale in the bones |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Lantern | Folk horror | Sealed room / monkey’s-paw bargain |
| 2 Well | Horror / names | Tam Lin, true names |
| 3 Watch | Classic warband | Wild Hunt, the dead company |
| 4 Class | Uncanny | Pied Piper |
| 5 Nail | Classic cursed craft | The gift that will not stay |
| 6 Flock | Folk horror | Wolf in sheep’s clothing |
| 7 Ward | Gothic | Sleep that is not sleep |

| 10 Quiet | Mythic | Orpheus / the thing the seals were feeding |

## Rules accuracy

2014 SRD or 2024 SRD 5.2.1, toggle, never mixed. Extra Attack is level 5. Paladin smite is on-hit in 2014 and a spell in 2024. No non-SRD subclasses.

## What we will not do

- Generate eight filler nights in one sitting.
- Copy Vecna, official adventures, or anything from Drive that we do not own.
- Require a BBEG.


## NPC stats

Do not print AC, HP, attacks, or a stat block beside dialogue or read-aloud. Name the NPC. Clicking the name opens the stat block. The full blocks live in an appendix at the end of the module. New adventures follow this. No exceptions.
