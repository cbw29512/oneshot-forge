# Right to OwlBear Arms v1.2 — Authoritative Repair Packet

**Status: v1.2 source-of-truth correction layer — 2026-10-05**

This document supersedes contradictory gameplay/story instructions in the preserved v1.1 PDF bundle. The original v1.1 ZIP remains unchanged for provenance. When this document conflicts with v1.1, **v1.2 wins**.

## Theme spine

A genius owlbear has a legitimate legal claim to the village's magic weapons while a raid deadline makes the argument immediately practical.

The legal dispute, investigation, preparation, and raid must remain one story. Negotiation changes the defense. The defense pays off the negotiation.

---

# 1. Locked chronology

Use this chronology everywhere.

### Three months ago — the logging-camp raid
Raiders attacked the north logging camp. **Tomas survived that raid.** He saw signs that someone inside Brindlebarrow had supplied the raiders with patrol information, but he did not know who.

Edra later learned that Tomas was asking questions. She feared exposure and began feeding him false leads.

### Two days ago — Cobb and Tomas enter Grubbleclaw's territory
Old Cobb and Tomas followed a fresh raider trail into the woods. They reached the edge of Grubbleclaw's lair while looking for a hidden approach the raiders might use.

Tomas discovered physical evidence connecting Edra to the raiders.

### Two days ago — Tomas dies in the woods
Edra's raider contact intercepted Tomas after he and Cobb separated near the lair. **Tomas died in the woods while carrying evidence of Edra's betrayal. He did not die in the logging-camp raid and Grubbleclaw did not kill him.**

Cobb escaped. He remembers leaving Tomas near the lair because that is the last place he saw him alive; that incomplete memory is why Cobb initially suspects the owlbear.

### Yesterday — Grubbleclaw takes possession
Grubbleclaw recovered abandoned village weapons and evidence from the contested woods and declared them held under his claimed territorial law until ownership and safety could be resolved.

### Today — the adventure
Brindlebarrow wants its weapons returned before the raiders arrive at sundown. The party investigates Tomas's death, Edra's betrayal, Grubbleclaw's claim, and the impending raid.

## Required clue interpretation

- Cobb is truthful when he says he last saw Tomas near the lair.
- Evidence at Tomas's death site shows humanoid/raider involvement, not owlbear mauling.
- Edra's motive remains: conceal collaboration and prevent Tomas from exposing her.
- Grubbleclaw's possession of the weapons is a legal/property dispute, not proof of Tomas's murder.

# 2. Four-hour preparation clock

Once the party returns from the Grubbleclaw negotiation or commits to defending Brindlebarrow, put **4 Preparation Hours** on the table.

Each preparation job lists its cost. Jobs may be performed in parallel by different PCs/NPCs unless they require the whole party.

## Defense Points

Brindlebarrow begins at **4 Defense Points (DP)**.

- Minimum 0.
- Maximum 6.
- Preparation can raise DP to 6.
- Raid failures reduce DP.
- Reaching **0 DP** is the loss threshold. Never use "below 0."

### Preparation jobs

| Job | Time | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Bess drills the militia | **2 hours** | +1 DP. This is the only militia-training DP bonus. |
| PCs fortify gates/lanes | **1 hour** | DC 13 group check using suitable Athletics, Survival, carpenter's tools, or mason's tools. Success +1 DP; failure consumes the hour but reveals the weak east lane. |
| Scout raider approach | **1 hour** | Learn the exact Wave 1 entry lane and gain Advantage on the party's first initiative roll at that front. No DP. |
| Establish signal plan | **1 hour** | Once during the defense, move one PC between fronts without losing an entire wave; that PC arrives at the end of the current round. |
| Relocate the eggs | **1 hour** | Requires Grubbleclaw's consent. Moves the nest objective to the village; the raid becomes a single-front defense. |
| Treat/arm militia | **1 hour** | The first time an undefended village wave would cost 2 DP, it costs only 1. |
| Rest | **1 hour** | A character may take a short rest if the table's rules allow it. |

**Stacking:** Bess training and PC fortification are different jobs and may both add DP. Multiple attempts at the same +DP job never stack.

# 3. Fronts, distance, initiative, and wave timing

The village gate and Grubbleclaw's nest are **a quarter-mile apart by the marked forest path**.

- Normal travel between fronts during the raid takes **one full wave**.
- A character using the prepared Signal Plan can make the trip once without losing a full wave, arriving at the end of the current round.
- Each wave lasts **3 combat rounds** or until its attackers break, surrender, flee, or are defeated.
- Wave 2 starts after a **1-round reset**.
- Wave 3 starts after another **1-round reset**.
- On Wave 3, raiders attempt the egg objective if the eggs remain at the nest.

Use one initiative order per active front. If the party is split, run one round at Village, then one round at Nest, alternating until both fronts complete that round.

# 4. Route procedures

## Route A — Alliance; party defends village
Grubbleclaw and two owl-bearer allies defend the nest. The party runs the full Village waves.

- Nest does not lose DP unless the party previously betrayed Grubbleclaw or exposed the nest route to Edra.
- On Wave 3, Grubbleclaw sends the signal that the egg raid has failed.

## Route B — Alliance; party defends nest
Grubbleclaw goes to the village with allied defenders. The party runs the Nest waves.

- Village uses current DP and loses **1 DP only if the party failed to complete either Bess Training or Fortification**.
- If both were completed, the allied village defense holds without DP loss.

## Route C — Split party
Recommended split: 3 PCs Village / 2 PCs Nest with Grubbleclaw.

Use the **Split Village** counts and full Nest counts below.

- Grubbleclaw is an allied NPC at the Nest.
- Never increase difficulty by an undefined "tier."
- Alternate rounds between fronts.

## Route D — Eggs relocated to village
There is only one active front.

- Run full Village waves.
- Wave 3 attackers include the Egg Team objective at the village instead of the nest.
- A PC adjacent to the egg enclosure can use an Action to secure it; while secured, raiders cannot seize an egg until they first force that PC away or incapacitate them.

## Route E — No alliance
Grubbleclaw does not assist Brindlebarrow.

- Party runs full Village waves.
- If eggs remain at the nest, the egg raid occurs offscreen; it does **not** reduce village DP.
- The aftermath records what happened to Grubbleclaw separately.
- Refusing alliance is not an automatic failure.

# 5. Defense Point loss

At the end of each wave, check the active front.

### Village
Lose **1 DP** if any of these are true:
- the wave's objective was not stopped;
- more than half the attackers remain active when Round 3 ends;
- the party abandons the front without allied coverage.

Lose **2 DP instead** if the Village is completely undefended and has no allied coverage.

### Nest
The nest uses objective state, not village DP.

- If attackers control the egg chamber at the end of Wave 3, one egg is taken.
- If all eggs were relocated, ignore this rule.
- Losing an egg changes the ending but does not retroactively change village DP.

## Complete DP outcomes

| Final DP | Outcome |
|---:|---|
| **6** | Decisive defense. Minimal structural damage; village openly credits the party and any alliance. |
| **5** | Strong defense. Minor damage; no lasting loss of services. |
| **4** | Hold. Noticeable damage, but the village remains fully functional. |
| **3** | Costly hold. One public structure is damaged and requires repair. |
| **2** | Severe hold. Several homes/shops damaged; recovery becomes a visible campaign consequence. |
| **1** | Barely held. Major damage and injuries; the village survives because the party prevented a complete breach. |
| **0** | Overrun. The party may still extract civilians, pursue raiders, or save the eggs, but Brindlebarrow loses the battle. |

There is no unlisted score.

# 6. Encounter budget and wave roster

Production Pass 1 sourcing: Raider Scout = SRD **Scout** reskin; Ash Berserker = SRD **Berserker** reskin; Raid Captain = SRD **Bandit Captain** reskin (longsword for scimitar, Command Push for Parry). Raider and Ash Shaman are original creatures built to CR 1/4 and CR 1 with DMG creature math. Grubbleclaw is an upgraded SRD **Owlbear** (about CR 4, 1,100 XP, if he ever fights the party). The XP below uses the 2014 DMG multipliers.

For five level-6 PCs, the audit baseline is:
- Easy 1,500
- Medium 3,000
- Hard 4,500
- Deadly 7,000

## Enemy XP values

- Raider — CR 1/4 / 50 XP
- Raider Scout — CR 1/2 / 100 XP
- Ash Berserker — CR 2 / 450 XP
- Ash Shaman — CR 1 / 200 XP
- Raid Captain — CR 2 / 450 XP

## Full Village waves

### Wave 1 — Probe
6 Raiders + 2 Raider Scouts = **500 raw XP**.
8 creatures → x2.5 encounter adjustment = **1,250 adjusted XP**.

### Wave 2 — Break the gate
4 Raiders + 2 Ash Berserkers = **1,100 raw XP**.
6 creatures → x2 adjustment = **2,200 adjusted XP**.

### Wave 3 — Command push
2 Raiders + 2 Ash Berserkers + 1 Ash Shaman + 1 Raid Captain = **1,650 raw XP**.
6 creatures → x2 adjustment = **3,300 adjusted XP** (between Medium and Hard for five PCs).

No single full-party village wave reaches the five-character Hard threshold.

## Split Village waves — 3 PCs
Use:
- Wave 1: 4 Raiders + 1 Scout = 300 raw; x2 = **600 adjusted**.
- Wave 2: 2 Raiders + 2 Berserkers = 1,000 raw; x2 = **2,000 adjusted**.
- Wave 3: 1 Raider + 1 Berserker + 1 Shaman + 1 Captain = 1,150 raw; x2 = **2,300 adjusted**.

For three level-6 PCs: Easy 900 / Medium 1,800 / Hard 2,700 / Deadly 4,200. Wave 3 sits between Medium and Hard. It is the intended split-front peak, and the waves come without a long rest.

## Nest waves — 2 PCs + Grubbleclaw ally
- Wave 1: 4 Raiders = 200 raw; x2 = **400 adjusted**.
- Wave 2: 2 Raiders + 2 Scouts = 300 raw; x2 = **600 adjusted**.
- Wave 3: 2 Raiders + 1 Berserker = 550 raw; x2 = **1,100 adjusted**.

For two level-6 PCs: Easy 600 / Medium 1,200 / Hard 1,800 / Deadly 2,800. Grubbleclaw is additional allied power, so these intentionally sit below Medium before his contribution.

## Party-size scaling (3–6 players)

The heroes packet has five characters. These rules let 3–6 players run the same procedure.
- **3 PCs:** use the *Split Village waves — 3 PCs* table above for the village. If the party defends the nest instead, use the Nest waves with Grubbleclaw. During preparation, Bess's militia completes *Treat/arm militia* on its own, with no PC time spent.
- **4 PCs** (Easy 1,200 / Medium 2,400 / Hard 3,600 / Deadly 5,600): remove 2 Raiders from every Full Village wave. Wave 1: 4 Raiders + 2 Scouts = 400 raw, x2 = 800. Wave 2: 2 Raiders + 2 Berserkers = 1,000 raw, x2 = 2,000. Wave 3: 2 Berserkers + Shaman + Captain = 1,550 raw, x2 = 3,100.
- **5 PCs:** use the Full Village waves as written.
- **6 PCs** (Easy 1,800 / Medium 3,600 / Hard 5,400 / Deadly 8,400; the DMG uses one multiplier step lower for six or more PCs): add 1 Raider Scout to Waves 1 and 2, and 1 Ash Berserker to Wave 3. Wave 1: 600 raw, x2 = 1,200. Wave 2: 1,200 raw, x1.5 = 1,800. Wave 3: 2,100 raw, x1.5 = 3,150.
- If you hand out a sixth character, use any level-6 SRD character. Do not raise DCs for a larger party; add enemies or fronts instead.

# 7. Complete enemy quick-run blocks

## Raider
**AC 13 · HP 11 · Speed 30 ft.** *(original, CR 1/4)*

STR +2, DEX +2, CON +1, INT +0, WIS +0, CHA +0.

**Scimitar.** Melee +4, 5 ft.; 1d6+2 slashing.

**Shortbow.** Ranged +4, 80/320 ft.; 1d6+2 piercing.

**Morale:** flees if isolated after its wave leader falls.

## Raider Scout
**AC 13 · HP 16 · Speed 30 ft.** *(SRD Scout, CR 1/2)*

DEX save +4; Perception +4; Stealth +6.

**Multiattack.** Two Shortsword attacks or two Longbow attacks.

**Shortsword.** Melee +4; 1d6+2 piercing.

**Longbow.** Ranged +4, 150/600 ft.; 1d8+2 piercing.

**Skirmish:** after making a ranged attack, the Scout may move 10 ft. without provoking from the target of that attack.

## Ash Berserker
**AC 13 · HP 67 · Speed 30 ft.** *(SRD Berserker, CR 2)*

STR save +5; CON save +5.

**Greataxe.** Melee +5; 1d12+3 slashing.

**Reckless.** Before its first attack on its turn, it may gain Advantage on melee attacks this turn; attacks against it then have Advantage until its next turn.

**Morale:** fights until below 20 HP, then withdraws if the Captain is down.

## Ash Shaman
**AC 12 · HP 27 · Speed 30 ft.** *(original, CR 1)*
Spell-like attack +5; save DC 13.

**Ash Bolt.** Ranged +5, 60 ft.; 2d8 fire.

**Cinder Veil (Recharge 5–6).** One 15-ft-radius area within 60 ft. becomes choking ash until the start of the Shaman's next turn. Creatures entering or starting there make DC 13 Constitution save; failure: Blinded until end of their turn. The area is heavily obscured.

**Ember Mend (2/day).** One ally within 30 ft. regains 2d8+3 HP.

**Targeting:** protects the Captain first, then targets clustered defenders. Never invent extra spell slots.

## Raid Captain
**AC 15 · HP 65 · Speed 30 ft.** *(SRD Bandit Captain, CR 2)*

STR save +5, Dex save +4, Wis save +3.

**Multiattack.** Three attacks: two Longsword and one Dagger, or two Longbow attacks.

**Longsword.** Melee +5; 1d8+3 slashing.

**Dagger.** Melee or thrown +5, 20/60 ft.; 1d4+3 piercing.

**Longbow.** Ranged +4, 150/600 ft.; 1d8+2 piercing.

**Command Push (1/round).** When a Raider within 30 ft. hits, the Captain may use a Reaction to let that Raider move up to 10 ft. without provoking.

**Morale:** if reduced below 20 HP after the Shaman falls, offers retreat in exchange for safe withdrawal.

No Monster Manual lookup or undefined "+stealth" is required.

**Magistrate Grubbleclaw**
Large monstrosity; intelligent neutral adjudicator/ally.

**AC 16 with Magistrate's Mantle; AC 15 without it · HP 68 · Speed 40 ft.**

STR +5, DEX +1, CON +3, INT +2, WIS +3, CHA +3.
Saves: Str +8, Wis +6.
Perception +6, Insight +6, Persuasion +6.

**Multiattack.** One Beak and one Claw.

**Beak.** +8 to hit; 1d10+5 piercing.

**Claw.** +8 to hit; 2d8+5 slashing.

**Javelin.** +8 to hit, 30/120 ft.; 1d6+5 piercing.

### Magistrate's Mantle — CUSTOM ADVENTURE ITEM
While worn, the mantle increases Grubbleclaw's AC from 15 to 16. Removing it changes only AC; **current and maximum HP do not change**.

### Ceremonial Shield — CUSTOM EQUIPMENT
If Grubbleclaw spends an Action to take up the shield, AC becomes 17 and he cannot use Claw as part of Multiattack while holding it. He may drop it freely.

# 9. Custom item procedures

These are adventure-specific tools. They are **not** the standard magic items with similar names.

## Cobb's Web Wand — CUSTOM
2 charges.

As an Action, choose a 20-ft square within 60 ft. Creatures in the area make DC 13 Dexterity saves or become Restrained by sticky webbing. A restrained creature can use an Action for DC 13 Strength to escape. Fire clears a 5-ft square.

- First activation works normally.
- Second activation works normally, then the wand cracks and becomes nonmagical.
- There is **no self-web random roll**.

## Cobb's Smoke Bottle — CUSTOM
1 use.

As an Action, create a 20-ft-radius heavily obscured smoke cloud centered on a point within 20 ft. The cloud lasts 1 minute or until dispersed by strong wind.

This is not a standard Eversmoking Bottle.

# 10. Negotiation deception rule

Being caught in a deliberate lie does **not** start combat "at disadvantage."

Instead:

> If Grubbleclaw catches a character knowingly lying during the legal negotiation, that character has Disadvantage on their **next Charisma ability check against Grubbleclaw before the end of that negotiation scene**.

No attack roll, saving throw, later scene, or other character inherits that Disadvantage.

A serious betrayal may make Grubbleclaw refuse alliance, but it does not automatically force combat unless the party attacks or threatens the eggs.
