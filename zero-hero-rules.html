Level-0 Rules Quick Reference

FROM ZERO TO HERO · DM TABLE REFERENCE

The Hearthcross Years

Level-0 homebrew only · normal 2014/2024 rules resume at graduation

1. Every child starts equal

STR 9 · DEX 9 · CON 9 · INT 9 · WIS 9 · CHA 9

Race/species identity is fixed. Class is not chosen yet.

2. Upbringing changes the dice

Every Upbringing Lean has exactly:

2 Favored → Advantage
2 Neutral → normal
2 Challenging → Disadvantage

3. Character Advantage

A specific bond, memory, mentor lesson, possession, relationship, scene feature, accepted risk/cost, or prior consequence can earn Character Advantage.

No acting skill required.

4. Advantage cancellation

Favored + Character Advantage = Advantage.

Neutral + Character Advantage = Advantage.

Challenging + Character Advantage = normal.

Advantage + Disadvantage always cancels. Never stack.

5. Resolve a Development Check

  1. Player describes approach and chooses STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA.
  2. Apply Upbringing lane.
  3. Decide whether specific fiction earns Character Advantage.
  4. Roll 1d20, or 2d20 keep high/low as appropriate.
  5. Add current ability modifier.
  6. Compare total to authored DC.
  7. Then assign the Level-0 outcome tier.

6. Outcome → development

OutcomeGrowth
Critical success+2 Flexible DP
Success+1 Flexible DP
Failure+1 off-path Lesson DP
Critical failure+1 Lesson DP + redirect 1 invested point when legal

7. Critical tier

The total still decides success or failure first.

Natural 20 + successful total → Critical Success.

Natural 1 + failed total → Critical Failure.

A natural 20 does not rescue a failed total.

8. Ability spending

9→10: 1
10→11: 1
11→12: 1
12→13: 1
13→14: 2
14→15: 2

Level-0 score cap: 15.

9. Graduation envelope

Completing all 21 base opportunities guarantees at least 27 point-buy value.

Absolute Level-0 ceiling: 30.

Critical-success growth may make 28–30 Heroic/house-rule arrays.

10. Shared Growth Beats

Every player gets exactly one Development Check before the beat closes.

Rotate who acts first. A success changes the situation but never removes another player's turn.

11. Competition placement

All entrants use the contest's same primary ability.

Rank by final check total → kept d20 → story-only d20 tiebreak.

Placement cards add no extra DP.

12. Failure moves play

Failure costs pride, time, resources, position, property, or relationships. Required progression continues.

Early chapters do not default to lethal consequences.

13. Homebrew stops at graduation

The six-9 start, Upbringing Lean, Development/Lesson Points, Level-0 critical tiers, scrapbook development ledger, and 27–30 envelope are homebrew.

Once the base array is frozen, use the chosen 2014 or 2024 legal rules for race/species mechanics, background, class, HP, saves, proficiencies, equipment, spells, feats, and future advancement unless another homebrew is explicitly named.

Scrapbook rule: the character sheet says who the character is now; the cards show how they became that person.
