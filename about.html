About

99¢ One-Shots sells complete original adventures built for busy DMs. Run from the browser or use the printable kit. Click a card, read the stage, make the table's choice, and keep moving.

Original 5E-compatible fantasy. Not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2.1 under CC-BY-4.0.

The Lantern Below is the permanent free starter. Paid adventures are 99¢ after they pass a real DM playtest, revision, delivery, and release audit. Bundles come later after enough individual adventures are proven.

One-Shot Forge is the runner and creation system behind the storefront. The random generator lives in Forge Lab; the product is the finished, tested adventure.
