From Zero to Hero

Run The Hearthcross Years

Begin around age 12 with six 9s, no class, and an ordinary life in Hearthcross. Seven yearly mini-adventures build a Development Point wallet through choices, success, failure, and hard lessons. Spend those points when you decide who the character is becoming.

Level-0 growth rule: everyone begins with the same six 9s, but a balanced Upbringing Lean gives Advantage on two abilities, Disadvantage on two, and leaves two neutral. Base success is intentionally uncommon. A character-grounded approach can earn Character Advantage. Success gives flexible development; failure teaches something different; critical success earns an extra point; critical failure can redirect one already-invested point into an unexpected ability. Completing all 21 base growth opportunities guarantees at least a 27-point-buy-equivalent base array. The Level-0 base array can never exceed 30 point-buy value, no matter how many critical successes occur. Failure changes the path, not the minimum power.

How Character Advantage is earned

Advantage rewards a specific approach that fits the character and the scene. It never requires acting talent, accents, or a long speech.

  • Use an established bond, fear, value, aspiration, relationship, mentor lesson, familiarity, possession, or earlier consequence.
  • Use a concrete feature of the scene or accept a meaningful cost or risk that makes the plan more credible.
  • Explain what the character actually does. A generic justification repeated every check does not qualify.

No roleplay Disadvantage: failing to earn Advantage leaves the normal hard check in place; it does not add a penalty.

Abilities growing through play

Every character begins with six 9s. Upbringing changes the dice, not the starting numbers: two abilities are Favored (Advantage), two are Neutral, and two are Challenging (Disadvantage). Character Advantage can improve a Neutral check or cancel a Challenging one, but never stacks into double Advantage. Flexible points may be spent on any ability; Lesson points are locked by failure.
