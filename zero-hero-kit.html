10-MINUTE FIRST-SESSION SETUP

Do this before reading Chapter 1 aloud

  1. Pick 4–6 players. Four is the default table.
  2. Give each player a starter child or the blank Growth Sheet. Tod, Merlon, Halley, and Mira are the default four.
  3. Lock race/species identity and choose 2014 or 2024 for each character. Class stays blank.
  4. Confirm all six ability scores begin at 9.
  5. Choose the child's Upbringing Lean. Each has 2 Favored, 2 Neutral, and 2 Challenging abilities.
  6. Hand each player a journal. Keep scrapbook/trophy cards nearby.
  7. Put the Hearthcross Player Map on the table. Keep the DM Map hidden.
  8. Open Spring Chore Day in the DM runner and begin. You do not need to prepare an encounter or invent a hook.

THE RULES IN ONE MINUTE

What a first-time buyer must know

Start equal

Every ability begins at 9. Race/species is identity; class is not chosen yet.

Upbringing changes odds

Each child has 2 Favored abilities, 2 Neutral, and 2 Challenging. Favored rolls with Advantage; Challenging with Disadvantage.

Play builds the sheet

Each of 21 Growth Beats creates a Development Card and development result.

Failure still builds you

Failure gives a Lesson Point instead of deleting growth. Critical failure can redirect where one invested point landed.

Bounded ending

Everyone who completes the campaign reaches at least 27 point-buy value and can never exceed 30.

RAW begins after Level 0

Once the homebrew base array freezes, the chosen 2014 or 2024 rules supply the legal Level-1 package.
